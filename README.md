# Web3 Research Portfolio

A curated collection of research notes, case studies, and templates focused on Web3, tokenization, protocol analysis, and the African/Global South context. This repository is organized to support structured research, documentation, and reusable workflows.

## Repository Structure

```text
web3-research-portfolio/
├── README.md
├── rwa-research/
│   ├── real-finance-case-study.md
│   └── tokenization-research-notes.md
├── protocol-research/
│   └── research-template.md
├── africa-global-south/
│   └── research-index.md
└── methodology/
    └── how-i-research-a-protocol.md
```

## Contents

### RWA Research
The `rwa-research/` folder contains research around real-world assets (RWA), tokenization, and the broader financial and regulatory implications of blockchain-based systems.

### Protocol Research
The `protocol-research/` folder includes templates and documentation frameworks for evaluating blockchain protocols, understanding architecture, and organizing findings.

### Africa / Global South
The `africa-global-south/` folder captures research centered on regional context, adoption patterns, and the intersection of Web3 with economic and institutional realities in Africa and the Global South.

### Methodology
The `methodology/` folder outlines how research is approached, including frameworks for analyzing protocols and organizing evidence-based conclusions.

## Purpose

This portfolio is intended to:

- document research and analysis work in a repeatable way
- maintain a structured archive of notes and case studies
- provide reusable templates for future investigations
- capture learning on protocol design, tokenization, and market structure

## How to Use

- Browse the folders to review research notes and case studies.
- Use `protocol-research/research-template.md` as a starting point for structured protocol analysis.
- Reference the methodology notes for a consistent research process.
- Add new findings, sources, and updates via pull requests or issue discussions.
